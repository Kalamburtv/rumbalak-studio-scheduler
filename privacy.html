Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Rumbalak Studio, based in Norway, operates this website and develops Rumbalak Studio Scheduler for its own Pinterest business account. This policy describes the public website and the planned desktop integration.

This website

These pages use no sign-in forms, analytics scripts, or advertising cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses for hosting and security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

The desktop scheduler

The integration is still in development. Once enabled, the account owner will authorize it through Pinterest. Board information is used to populate the board picker. The selected preview image, title, description, and destination link are sent to Pinterest when the owner’s scheduled Pin is published. Pinterest handles that content under its privacy policy.

The planned integration keeps its authorization tokens and publication queue on the account owner’s computer. The public website does not receive tokens, account credentials, purchase records, or names and contact details of Pinterest users. The scheduler is designed around the studio’s own Pins and does not read messages or operate ad campaigns.

Access and retention

The owner will be able to revoke the scheduler’s access from Pinterest. Local drafts and schedule records can be removed by the owner. GitHub and Pinterest set their own retention periods for the information they process.

Contact

For questions about this policy or requests about your personal data, email rumbalakstudio@gmail.com. Rumbalak Studio uses your email address and message to respond to your request.

You can raise a privacy concern with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.
