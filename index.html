Rumbalak Studio Scheduler

A desktop tool in development for preparing and scheduling original image Pins for Rumbalak Studio’s repair shop templates.

Rumbalak Studio creates editable intake forms, service price lists, and invoice and warranty forms for independent repair shops. The scheduler is for the studio’s own Pinterest business account.

How the scheduler will work

Prepare
Build a draft from an original preview image, a short description, and a link to its product page.
Review
The account owner reviews each draft and chooses its board and publication time.
Publish
After Pinterest approves public API access, the desktop tool will publish the selected Pin at its scheduled time.

Account access

The owner will grant access through Pinterest’s authorization screen. The integration is designed to read boards and publish the Pins the owner selects.
